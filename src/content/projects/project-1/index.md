Lift is a training planner and workout tracker for people who write their own programs. Instead of separating planning, workout logging, and analytics across different tools, Lift keeps the entire training cycle connected.

Build reusable multi-week blocks on desktop, follow each workout on your phone with previous performance and rest timing, then review personal records, strength and volume trends, and effective sets by muscle group.
